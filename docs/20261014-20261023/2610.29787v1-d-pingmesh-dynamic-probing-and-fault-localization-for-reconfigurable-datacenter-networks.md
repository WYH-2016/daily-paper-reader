## 摘要
Pingmesh 通过服务器间周期性 ping 探测来监控数据中心网络。在可重构数据中心网络中，拓扑工程（TPE）与流量工程（TE）使得服务器对之间的均匀探测效率低下：固定 ping 列表既浪费探测预算于重复测试共用路径，又遗漏低概率经过的接口。频繁的控制平面更新进一步要求 ping 列表快速刷新，并在控制平面边界处引发路径歧义，从而影响故障定位精度。本文提出 D-Pingmesh——一种与 TPE/TE 控制平面协同设计的探测与故障定位系统。其控制平面感知的生成器基于当前 TPE/TE 快照，以贪心策略选取能最大化接口覆盖度的探测任务，构建高任务效率的 ping 列表；其增量式重放与修复机制在拓扑重配置时更新 ping 列表，而非从头重新计算；在控制平面边界处，Rho-EM 定位器通过 EM 风格迭代联合推断各探测任务中旧路径与新路径的混合权重以及故障集合。我们在一个含 8 个 POD 的可重构拓扑上对 D-Pingmesh 进行动态 TPE/TE 场景评估：在 0.99 覆盖率目标下，D-Pingmesh 所需探测任务数仅为 Pingmesh 的 1/17.7，且生成每个 ping 列表的速度约为 RD-Probe 的 5.1 倍；在 ns-3 仿真中，边界感知定位方法使微平均 F1 分数从基线 deTector PLL 的 0.871 提升至 0.953；真实测试平台部署结果表明，D-Pingmesh 可在秒级完成故障定位，且主机开销较低。上述结果证实，D-Pingmesh 是一种面向控制平面感知监控的、适用于可重构数据中心网络的实用化方案。

## Abstract
Pingmesh monitors datacenter networks through periodic pings between servers. In reconfigurable datacenter networks, topology engineering (TPE) and traffic engineering (TE) make uniform server-pair probing inefficient: a pinglist wastes budget retesting common paths while missing low-probability interfaces. Frequent control-plane updates further require rapid pinglist refresh, and cause path ambiguity at control-plane boundaries for fault localization. We present D-Pingmesh, a probing and fault-localization system co-designed with the TPE/TE control plane. Its control-plane-aware generator builds task-efficient pinglists by greedily admitting probes with high interface coverage under the current TPE/TE snapshot. An incremental replay-and-repair mechanism updates pinglists across reconfigurations rather than recomputing from scratch. At control-plane boundaries, a Rho-EM localizer jointly infers per-task old/new-path mixture weights and the fault set through EM-style iteration. We evaluate D-Pingmesh on an 8-pod reconfigurable topology under dynamic TPE/TE. At a 0.99 coverage target, D-Pingmesh uses 17.7x fewer tasks than Pingmesh and generates each pinglist about 5.1x faster than RD-Probe. In ns-3 simulation, boundary-aware localization improves micro F1 from 0.871 to 0.953 over the deTector PLL baseline. A testbed deployment demonstrates second-scale fault localization with low host overhead. These results demonstrate that D-Pingmesh is practical for reconfigurable datacenter networks with control-plane-aware monitoring.
