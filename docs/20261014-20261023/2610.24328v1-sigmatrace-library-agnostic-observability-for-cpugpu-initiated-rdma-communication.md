## 摘要
RDMA 网络技术可在大规模系统中实现低延迟、高带宽的通信。尽管该技术已被广泛采用，其可观测性却严重不足。现有工具或仅针对特定通信库，或仅报告聚合统计数据，无法反映单个操作的行为细节；其中 GPU 发起的网络通信可观测性缺口尤为显著——其内核态 RDMA 操作对主机完全不可见。本文提出 SigmaTrace，一种与通信库无关的 RDMA 性能分析框架，可为 CPU 和 GPU 发起的 RDMA 通信提供细粒度、逐操作级别的可观测性。SigmaTrace 在 libibverbs/mlx5dv 层（InfiniBand 与 RoCE 上各类通信库的共同底层）实施跟踪，并采用一种低开销的内存插桩技术；据我们所知，这是首个无需修改源码或二进制插桩即可实现 GPU 发起 RDMA 可观测性的方法。主机端跟踪在 128 卡 NCCL AllReduce 场景下引入 1.3% 的性能开销，在 1024 卡 RCCL 场景下开销低于 2%；在持久化内核的广度优先搜索（BFS）与混合专家（MoE）推理任务中，SigmaTrace 揭示了现有分析器无法观测到的内核态屏障扩散现象及 2.13 倍的跨节点流量倾斜。

## Abstract
RDMA networking enables low-latency, high-bandwidth communication on large systems. Despite its adoption, however, observability is lacking. Existing tools target specific communication libraries or only report aggregated data, missing per-operation behavior. The gap is widest for GPU-initiated networking, whose in-kernel RDMA operations are invisible to the host.   We present SigmaTrace, a library-agnostic RDMA profiling framework providing fine-grained, per-operation visibility for CPU and GPU-initiated networking. It traces operations at the libibverbs/mlx5dv layers, the common denominator beneath communication libraries on InfiniBand and RoCE, and employs a low-overhead memory interposition technique that, to our knowledge, gives the first observability into GPU-initiated RDMA without source patching or binary instrumentation.   Host-side tracing adds 1.3% overhead on NCCL AllReduce at 128 GPUs and under 2% on RCCL at 1024 GPUs. On a persistent-kernel BFS and Mixture-of-Experts inference, SigmaTrace exposes an in-kernel barrier spread and 2.13x inter-node traffic skew invisible to existing profilers.
